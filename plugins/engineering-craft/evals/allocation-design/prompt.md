Rules: an engineer only covers sites in their own region, priority sites need one dedicated engineer, some sites need an engineer with a specific certification, and nobody may drive more than 45 minutes to a site. We want to see what happens to coverage if the limit drops to 30 minutes, and show management the trade-offs. Design the approach. Design only, no code files.
